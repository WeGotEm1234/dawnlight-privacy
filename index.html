Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

Dawnlight is a sleep sound, nightlight and sunrise alarm app for iPhone. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Dawnlight does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking tools in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your alarms and preferences (such as your chosen sound, volume, light colour and brightness) are saved only on your iPhone so the app remembers them. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this information.

Permissions

  • Alarms: if you allow it, Dawnlight schedules backup alarms through iOS so you still wake up when the app is closed. These alarms are managed by iOS on your device.
  • Audio: Dawnlight plays sounds in the background so they continue while your screen is locked. It does not use the microphone.

Children

Dawnlight does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
